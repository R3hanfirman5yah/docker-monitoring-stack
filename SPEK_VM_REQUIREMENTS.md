# 🖥️ SPESIFIKASI VM UNTUK DOCKER MONITORING STACK\n\n## 📊 Rekomendasi Spesifikasi Hardware\n\n### **MINIMUM SPEC (Untuk Testing/Development)**\n```\nCPU:        2 vCores\nRAM:        4 GB\nStorage:    30 GB (SSD)\nNetwork:    100 Mbps\n```\n\n**⚠️ Catatan:** Hanya untuk testing atau traffic ringan. Tidak cocok untuk production.\n\n---\n\n### **RECOMMENDED SPEC (Untuk Production - 50-500 servers)**\n```\nCPU:        4-8 vCores\nRAM:        8-16 GB\nStorage:    100-200 GB (SSD)\nNetwork:    1 Gbps\n```\n\n**✅ Cocok untuk:**\n- Monitoring 50-500 servers\n- Log volume sedang (1-10 GB/hari)\n- Retention 7-30 hari\n- Traffic network menengah\n\n---\n\n### **HIGH PERFORMANCE SPEC (Untuk Enterprise)**\n```\nCPU:        16+ vCores\nRAM:        32-64 GB\nStorage:    500 GB - 2 TB (SSD)\nNetwork:    10 Gbps\n```\n\n**✅ Cocok untuk:**\n- Monitoring 500+ servers\n- Log volume tinggi (50+ GB/hari)\n- Retention 30-90 hari\n- High availability & clustering\n\n---\n\n## 🔍 Breakdown per Component\n\n### **1. PORTAINER** (Docker Management)\n```\nCPU:        0.1 vCore\nRAM:        256 MB\nStorage:    1 GB\n```\n**Fungsi:** Dashboard management Docker\n**Resource Usage:** Very Light ✅\n\n---\n\n### **2. PROMETHEUS** (Time Series Database)\n```\nCPU:        1-2 vCores\nRAM:        2-4 GB\nStorage:    50-200 GB (tergantung retention & metrics)\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:**\n- CPU: Sedang (scraping & querying)\n- Memory: Tinggi (cache metrics)\n- Storage: Sangat Tinggi (TSDB menyimpan semua data)\n\n**Storage Calculation:**\n```\n1 metric = ~2 bytes/sample\n1000 metrics × 60 samples/hari = 120 KB/hari\n\nUntuk 10,000 metrics:\n10,000 × 120 KB/hari = 1.2 GB/hari\n30 hari retention = 36 GB\n```\n\n---\n\n### **3. LOKI** (Log Storage)\n```\nCPU:        0.5-1 vCore\nRAM:        1-2 GB\nStorage:    20-100 GB (tergantung log volume)\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:**\n- CPU: Ringan (indexing logs)\n- Memory: Sedang (buffer)\n- Storage: Tinggi (compressed logs)\n\n**Storage Calculation:**\n```\n100 MB log/hari × 30 hari retention = 3 GB\n1 GB log/hari × 30 hari retention = 30 GB\n10 GB log/hari × 30 hari retention = 300 GB\n```\n\n---\n\n### **4. PROMTAIL** (Log Collector)\n```\nCPU:        0.2-0.5 vCore\nRAM:        256-512 MB\nStorage:    Minimal (hanya buffer)\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:** Very Light ✅\n\n---\n\n### **5. GRAFANA** (Visualization)\n```\nCPU:        0.5-1 vCore\nRAM:        512 MB - 1 GB\nStorage:    2-5 GB (untuk dashboards & databases)\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:** Light ✅\n\n---\n\n### **6. NODE EXPORTER** (System Metrics)\n```\nCPU:        0.1 vCore\nRAM:        50 MB\nStorage:    Minimal\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:** Very Light ✅\n\n---\n\n### **7. cADVISOR** (Container Monitoring)\n```\nCPU:        0.2-0.5 vCore\nRAM:        256-512 MB\nStorage:    Minimal\n```\n\n**Resource Usage:** Light ✅\n\n---\n\n## 💾 TOTAL RESOURCE CALCULATION\n\n### **Scenario 1: Monitoring
